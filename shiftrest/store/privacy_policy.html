🌙 ShiftRest

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

The short version: ShiftRest stores everything on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking, no ads. Your sleep data is yours.

1. What data does ShiftRest collect?

ShiftRest collects no data from you. The app does not connect to any server, does not require an account, and does not transmit any information over the internet.

All data you enter — including your name, job type, sleep goal, shift schedule, sleep logs, and sleep plans — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.

2. What is stored on your device?

  • Your profile (name, job type, sleep goal) — stored in SQLite
  • Your shift schedule — stored in SQLite
  • Your sleep logs — stored in SQLite
  • Your generated sleep plans — stored in SQLite
  • App preferences (dark mode, notification settings) — stored in SharedPreferences

None of this data leaves your device under any circumstances.

3. Notifications

ShiftRest can schedule local notifications (bedtime reminders, wake-up alarms, shift reminders). These are processed entirely on your device by the Android operating system. No notification data is transmitted externally.

Notification permission is requested the first time you use the sleep planner — not on app launch.

4. Third-party services

ShiftRest uses the following open-source packages, none of which collect or transmit user data:

  • Google Fonts — fonts may be downloaded from Google's CDN on first use if not cached. This is standard Android font rendering behaviour.
  • sqflite — local SQLite database library
  • flutter_local_notifications — local notification scheduling
  • fl_chart — chart rendering library (no network access)

5. Children's privacy

ShiftRest does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. As no data is collected by the app, COPPA and similar regulations do not apply.

6. Data deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by going to Settings → Clear all data. This permanently removes your profile, shift schedule, sleep logs, and all generated plans from the device.

Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to this policy

If ShiftRest ever introduces features that involve data collection or external services, this privacy policy will be updated and users will be notified via an in-app notice.

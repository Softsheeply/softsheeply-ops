## App Title (max 50 chars)
ShiftRest: Sleep Planner for Shift Work

## Short Description (max 80 chars)
Sleep smarter around your shifts. Built for nurses, drivers & night workers.

## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Standard sleep apps tell you to wind down at 9pm. You're starting your shift at 9pm.

**ShiftRest is built for the people who work while the world sleeps.**

Nurses. Warehouse workers. Truck drivers. Factory staff. Paramedics. Security guards. If your sleep schedule is at the mercy of rotating shifts, this app was made for you.

---

### 🌙 SHIFT-AWARE SLEEP PLANNING

Tell ShiftRest your schedule and it calculates exactly when to sleep — automatically adjusting for night shift prep, post-shift recovery, rotating weeks, and days off.

No guesswork. Just: "Sleep from 8:30 AM → 4:00 PM."

---

### ⚡ SHIFT PATTERN TEMPLATES

Working a continental rotation? 4 on 4 off? NHS 3-shift pattern?

Pick your rotation and ShiftRest fills your schedule for weeks ahead — no manual entry. Supported patterns include:
• Continental (2 days, 2 nights, 4 off)
• 4 On 4 Off (nights and days)
• NHS 3-Shift
• 5 On 2 Off
• 3 On 3 Off
• 5 nights on 5 off
• Custom patterns

---

### ☕ CAFFEINE CUTOFF CALCULATOR

Know exactly when to stop drinking coffee based on your sleep window, not some generic 2pm rule. Adjusts for your personal caffeine sensitivity.

---

### 💡 LIGHT & DARKNESS GUIDE

Light is the most powerful signal for your body clock. ShiftRest tells you:
• When to seek bright light exposure
• When to block light before your sleep window
• The science behind blackout curtains, blue light glasses, and phone habits

---

### 🔄 3-DAY RECOVERY PLANNER

Just finished a stretch of night shifts? Enter how many you worked and get a personalised 3-day recovery plan that walks your sleep schedule back to normal without destroying your rhythm.

---

### 🌿 MELATONIN TIMING

Most shift workers take melatonin at the wrong dose and the wrong time. ShiftRest calculates exactly when to take it (low-dose 0.5mg vs higher doses) based on your target sleep window and shift type.

---

### 📊 SLEEP DEBT TRACKER

See your running sleep deficit vs goal for the week — in hours. Know whether you're behind or ahead, and by how much.

---

### 📈 SLEEP HISTORY & CHARTS

Log each night's sleep (including split sleep sessions) with a quality rating. See 14 days of sleep duration and quality trends in clean charts. Swipe to delete old entries.

---

### 🔔 SMART REMINDERS

Set notifications for:
• Bedtime (15, 30, or 60 minutes early)
• Wake-up alarm
• Shift reminder (1, 2, or 3 hours before)
• Caffeine cutoff alert

---

### 🔒 100% PRIVATE

No account. No login. No data leaves your phone. Everything stored locally on your device with SQLite.

---

**Free. No ads. No subscriptions.**

Built by someone who knows the feeling of getting the scheduling advice wrong at 4am and paying for it the next day.
